## The entry

**laternever** — the video you save to watch later and never open again.

_68 characters, one line._

## Why this word

Everyone keeps a pile of videos, posts, and articles saved "for later" — and never opens them. English has words for the act of saving, but no single word for the saved thing itself. *laternever* fills that gap: it fuses the promise (*later*) with the outcome (*never*), and it is easy to say, spell, and remember.
